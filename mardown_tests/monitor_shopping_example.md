# Monitor shopping example

The monitor to buy under £500 is the **ViewSonic VG2756-4K**, at **£381.93** on Amazon, with 3 left and free UK delivery. Checkout stays with you.

It is a 27-inch 4K IPS monitor with two HDMI inputs, a stand depth of **202 mm**, and **100% sRGB coverage**. That depth is under the Alienware AW2725Q’s 210 mm, by 8 mm. It also has a 100×100 mm VESA mount, so an arm can take the stand off the desk entirely. The Fit auditor accepted it: Amazon and ViewSonic’s spec sheet agree on size, HDMI, the 202 mm depth, and the price.

The next row is the **MSI PRO MP273U** at **£138.98** from Scan. It is also 27-inch 4K IPS, with a shallower 181 mm stand and **99% sRGB overlap**. One coverage point separates them, which is why the locked sort names the ViewSonic. Say if you want the MSI instead.

## How this was locked

Screen size is 25 to 27 inches, with 27 preferred. HDMI is required. The stand must be strictly under 210 mm deep, which is the AW2725Q’s depth with its stand. The main list is under £500. A separate list runs from £500 to £1500. Both routes were searched: Amazon and eBay, then manufacturer and independent retailers. Preferences such as resolution and colour were applied only after the lists existed.

One row was dropped. The BenQ EW270Q’s own sheet lists a depth of 211.15 mm.
